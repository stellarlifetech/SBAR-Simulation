SBAR Simulation: Sound Library

Every sound in the simulation: 30 provider voice recordings (6 providers × 5 lines) and 11 game sound effects.

Game sound effects

Generated live in the browser, so they sound the same on every device. Played at the same loud, clear volume as in the game.

  • Ringback (nurse calling)What the nurse hears while the provider's phone rings (rings up to 3 times).
  • Incoming call (provider calling)The provider calls back with orders.
  • PickupThe provider answers.
  • Message sentA script line goes out.
  • Incoming messageThe provider replies.
  • Hang-upThe call ends.
  • Correct answerA station answer locks in correctly.
  • Message flowingThe token moves to the next teammate.
  • LadderPerfect station bonus.
  • SnakeA slip at a station.
  • Pipeline finishedThe group completes the pipeline.

Provider voices

Recorded with natural neural voices, gender-matched to each provider and loudness-normalized so they stay clear on classroom speakers.
